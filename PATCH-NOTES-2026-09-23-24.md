## What you can use now

- **Ask Adam from every tab.** Adam keeps the working tab, selected system or toon, recent scans, and relevant fleet context in view. Doctrine Market questions about which item to buy, estimated return, stock, budget, or a named item use JLR's own market snapshot. Answers include the estimated gross spread and warn when source data is old or incomplete. [Grounded answers](https://github.com/JohnLuman/JLR-Miner-Tracker/commit/e7a1f62) · [Global Adam access](https://github.com/JohnLuman/JLR-Miner-Tracker/commit/aab7466)
- **Review mining and payout more clearly.** The six top metrics share one bar; app-wide payout shows linked-toon coverage and the UTC EVE-day reset. Valid T3 ore contributes to payout even if mined outside a tracked field. Ledger caches survive restarts, and available fleet totals continue to display while an individual toon is unavailable. [Payout accounting](https://github.com/JohnLuman/JLR-Miner-Tracker/commit/09b9fe6) · [Durable cache](https://github.com/JohnLuman/JLR-Miner-Tracker/commit/b2367ed) · [Summary bar](https://github.com/JohnLuman/JLR-Miner-Tracker/commit/181ef99)
- **Inspect fleet sessions after mining.** Fleet Performance focuses on assigned miners, actual versus fitted targets, useful per-miner metrics, distinct trend charts, neutral variance language, and explicit states when data is missing. [Assigned miners](https://github.com/JohnLuman/JLR-Miner-Tracker/commit/2e69367) · [Post-mining review](https://github.com/JohnLuman/JLR-Miner-Tracker/commit/f4270eb) · [Data states](https://github.com/JohnLuman/JLR-Miner-Tracker/commit/0477a37)
- **Use regional intel and gas tracking.** Hot Zones supports region selection, comparable signals, cached regional data, and an hourly region list. Gas includes wormhole signatures and regional views; the location-aware nearest mining system shortcut is accessible through Adam. [Hot Zones](https://github.com/JohnLuman/JLR-Miner-Tracker/commit/e3e6e97) · [Wormhole gas](https://github.com/JohnLuman/JLR-Miner-Tracker/commit/604a215) · [Nearest mining system](https://github.com/JohnLuman/JLR-Miner-Tracker/commit/7c767d6)
- **Find controls with less clutter.** Toons now presents connection and ESI status more clearly; routine views hide companion setup and advanced sync tools. Feedback is simpler and attaches safe context automatically, while the verified owner can read complete feedback messages. [Toons](https://github.com/JohnLuman/JLR-Miner-Tracker/commit/8b2eede) · [Feedback](https://github.com/JohnLuman/JLR-Miner-Tracker/commit/35012e3) · [Owner view](https://github.com/JohnLuman/JLR-Miner-Tracker/commit/329c06f)

## Payout-card follow-up

The new EVE-day message now uses two short lines for the reset time and yesterday's m³/ISK totals. Its card grows to contain both lines, including in Compact mode, and the exact previous UTC date remains available in the subtitle tooltip. Browser asset versions changed so an existing tab receives the fix on reload.

**Current UI note:** Adam is a text assistant. The experimental live microphone and the short-lived ESS, CRAB, and interference watches listed in the change trail are no longer present.
